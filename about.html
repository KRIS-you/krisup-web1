About KRIS

KmicRo Industrial Service

A professional custom cooling components and precision machining service provider.

KmicRo Industrial Service (Brand: KRIS, www.krisup.com) is a professional custom cooling components and precision machining service provider, focusing on cooling field and precision structural parts manufacturing. Backed by a veteran team with rich experience in thermal design, precision processing and cross-border supply chain, we connect global buyers with the top manufacturing resources of the Yangtze River Delta.

We specialize in custom copper & aluminum heat sinks and high-precision metal/non-metal machined parts that meet international thermal and dimensional standards, striving to be the most reliable partner for global industrial clients.

We assist clients in reviewing and checking drawings, and propose constructive solutions to seek easy processing and effective cost control. We offer precise sheet metal processing and precision machining services, with 3D modeling and thermal simulation to provide professional advice. Our inspection equipment includes high-precision optical microscopes, roughness testers, flatness testers, and 2D/3D measurement systems.

We maintain strict confidentiality regarding our clients' product information. We fully recognize that such information constitutes our clients' intellectual property or trade secrets, and therefore we will not disclose any of their trade secrets to third parties.

Our Mission

To be the most reliable custom cooling and precision machining partner for global industrial clients, providing high-performance, cost-effective thermal solutions and structural parts with consistent quality.
